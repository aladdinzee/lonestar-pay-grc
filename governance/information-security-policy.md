# Information Security Policy

**Document Owner:** CISO
**Approved By:** CEO
**Version:** 1.0(Draft)
**Effective Date:** Simulated Date
**Review Cycle:** Annual

**Purpose**

To establish Lonestar Pay's commitment to protecting confidentiality, integrity, and availability of customer, employee and company information.

**Scope**

Applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access Lonestar Pay systems or data.

**Management Commitment**

Lonestar Pay leadership supports the information security program and provides resources to maintain it. Security is a responsibility shared by everyone at the comapny.

**Related Policies**

- Access Control Policy
- Change Management Policy
- Incident Response Policy

**Roles & Responsibilities**

- CEO : Sponsors the program and approves this policy
- CISO : Owns the program and this policy
- GRC Analyst : Maintains the policies and tracks acknowledgements and training.
- All users : Follow this policy and report concerns.

**Policy Statements**

1. Data classification : All data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted, and handled according to its level. eg : Customer Financial Data is Confidential.

2. Encryption : Confidential and Restricted data must be encryption both at rest and in transit.

3. Security Awareness Training : All employees complete security training at hire and annually. Completion is recorded.

4. Policy acknowledgement : All employees and contractors acknowledge security policies at hire and annually. Acknowledgements are recorded.

5. Acceptable use of AI : Only company-approved AI tools may be used with Lonestar Pay data. Confidential or Restricted data must not be entered into unapproved AI tools.

6. Reporting : Suspected policy violations or incidents must be reported under the Incident Response Policy.

**Exceptions**

Exceptions require written CISO Approval, and an expiration date.

**Enforcement**

Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

**Review**

This policy is reviewed at least annually.
