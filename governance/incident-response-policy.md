# Incident Response Policy

**Document Owner:** CISO
**Approved By:** CEO
**Version:** 1.0(Draft)
**Effective Date:** Simulated Date
**Review Cycle:** Annually

**Purpose**

To ensure that Lonestar Pay detects, responds to, and learns from security incidents in a consistent and timely way, limiting harm to customers and the business.

**Scope**

Applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties, and to all in-scope systems, including AI fraud model and AI support chatbot.

**Definitions**

- Event : any observable occurence in the system or network.
- Incident : An event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of systems or data.

**Roles & Responsibilities**

- CISO : Incident commander who directs the response.
- Legal : Decides on customer, regulator, and partner notification.
- Head of Engineering : Leads technical containment and recovery.
- HR : Supports incidents involving employees.
- GRC Analyst : Maintains incident records and tracks follow-up actions.
- All employees : report suspected incidents.

**Severity Levels**

- Low : Minor policy violation, no exposed data. eg : lost badge, reported quickly.

- Medium : Limited Impact, contained. eg : Phishing email clicked, no data is accesssed.

- High : Confirmed unauthorized access or severe impact. eg : Comprimsed employee account.

- Critical : Customer Data exposed or major outage. eg : Database breach.

**Policy Statements**

1. Reporting : Suspected incidents must be reported to the security team immediately, and no later than one hour of discovery.

2. Triage : The security team assigns a severity level and incident owner.

3. Containment & Recovery : The Incident Commander directs the containment, eradication, and recovery actions.

4. Notification : Legal determines whether and when customers, regulators, or partners must be notified, based on applicable laws and contract terms.

5. Documentation : Every incident is recorded, including timeline, severity, actions taken, and outcome.

6. Post-incident Review : A blameless review is held after every Medium-or-higher incident, with corrective actions assigned and tracked to completion.

7. Testing : The incident response process is tested at least annually through a tabletop exercise.

**Exceptions**

Exceptions require a CISO approval.

**Enforcement**

Failure to report incidents may result in disciplinary action.

**Review**

This policy is reviewed at least annually after and after every Critical Incident.
