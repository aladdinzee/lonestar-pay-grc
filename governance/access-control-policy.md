# Access Control Policy

**Document Owner:** CISO
**Approved By:** CEO
**Version:** 1.0(draft)
**Effective Date:** Simulated date
**Review Cycle:** Annual

**Purpose**

To ensure that access to Lonestar Pay systems and data is limited to authorized individuals and is appropriate to their job responsibilities.

**Scope**

Applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties accessing in-scope systems: AWS, Okta, Github, Google Workspace, Slack, AI Fraud Model & AI Customer Chat support.

**Roles & Responsibilities**

- CISO : Owns this policy and approves exceptions.
- IT Admin : provisions, reviews, and remove access
- Managers : requests access appropriate to their team's role.
- All users : protect credentials and report suspected misuse.
- GRC Analyst : verifies compliance and retains evidence.

**Policy Statements**

1. Multi Factor Authentication : MFA is required for all users on all in-scope systems.

2. Role-Based Access : Access is granted based on the job role and principle of least privilege. Access outside the user's role requires documented manager and system-owner approval.

3. Access Reviews : IT performs access reviews of all in-scope systems quarterly. Results and corrections are documented and retained.

4. Offboarding : Access is removed within 24 hour of an employee's or contractor's departure.

5. Privileged Accounts : Administrators use separate, dedicated admin accounts for privileged tasks. Admin account activity is logged.

**Exceptions**

Exceptions require written CISO approval, a documented business reason, and an expiration date.

**Enforcement**

Violations may result in access removal and disciplinary action, up to and including termination

**Review**

This policy is reviewed at least annually, or after signifant changes.
