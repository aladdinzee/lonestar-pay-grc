# Risk Assessment Methodology

**Document Owner:** CISO
**Approved By:** CEO
**Version:** 1.0(Draft)
**Review Cycle:** Annual

**Purpose**

To define a consistent, repeatable method for identifying, scoring, and treating information security risks at Lonestar Pay.

**Process**

1. Identify the Aseets
2. Identify the threats and vulnerabilities for each assets.
3. Score likelihood and impact.
4. Calculate risk scoring and rating.
5. Choose a treatment plan (mitigate, transfer, accept, avoid)
6. Record in the risk register and review on schedule.

**Likelihood Scale**

- 1 : Rare (Not expected to occur in next 12 months)

- 2 : Unlikely (Could occur once in several years)

- 3 : Possible (Could occur within next 12 months)

- 4 : Likely (Expected to occur at least once a year)

- 5 : Almost certain (Expected to occur multiple times a year)

**Impact**

- 1 : Minimal (<$5k, no cutomer effect)

- 2 : Minor ($5k-$25k, few customer briefly affected)

- 3 : Moderate ($25k-$100k, many customers affected)

- 4 : Major ($100k-$200k, customer data exposed)

- 5 : Severe (>$200k, widespread data losee or outage)

**Risk Score & Rating**

Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact

- 1-5 : Low
- 6-12 : Medium
- 13-19 : High
- 20-25 : Critical

**Treatment Options**

- Mitigate : reduce the likelihood or impact with controls.

- Accept : knowlingly retain the risk.

- Transfer : Share the risk through insurance or contract terms.

- Avoid : Stop the activity that is causing the risk.

**Risk Appetite & Acceptance Authority**

- Low : Risk Owner

- Medium : CISO

- High : CEO, Advised by CISO

- Critical : Not acceptable (must be mitigated, transferred, or avoided)

**Review**

The risk register is reviewed quarterly, and after any major incident or significant change.
