# AI Use Policy

**Document Owner:** CISO
**Approved by:** CEO
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** Simulated Date
**Review Cycle:** Annual

**Purpose**

To ensure AI systems built, bought or operated by Lonestar Pay are safe, accountable, explainable, and used in line with legal and contractual obligations.

**Scope**

Applies to All AI systems that Lonestar builds, configures, or relies on, including the AI fraud model, the AI support chatbot, and third party AI services. Employee use of AI tools is covered by Information Security Policy.

**Roles & Responsibilities**

- CISO : Owns this policy and co-approves new AI uses.
- Legal : Reviews AI uses for privacy, consumer protection and contract risk.
- Head of Engineering : Owns the technical operation of AI systems.
- AI system owner : A named person accountable for each AI system.
- GRC Analyst : Maintains the AI risk register and tracks monitoring and review evidence.

**Policy Statements**

1. AI register : Lonestar maintains a register of every AI system, with its purpose, owner, data used, risk level, and any third party providers.

2. Human Oversight : Merchants may appeal an automated fraud decision to a human reviewer. A sample of automated decisions, including approvals, is reviewed at least monthly. 

3. Approval of New AI uses : No AI system may be launched or materially expanded without a documented risk review and approval by CISO and Legal Teams.

4. Transparency : Customer-facing AI must clearly state that it is AI and provide a way to reach a human.

5. Monitoring : Each AI system is monitored for accuracy, error rates, and drift. Tresholds are defined for each systems, and breaches trigger an alert and a documented review.

6. Data use : Customer data is not used to train AI models without document approval from Legal. Third party AI providers must disclose whether Lonestar data is used or training.

7. Changes : Changes to AI systems must follow the Change Management Policy, including validation requirements of that policy.

8. Incidents : AI Failures, such as harmful decisions or data exposure through prompts, are handled as incidents under the Incident Response Policy.

**Exceptions**

Exceptions require written CISO approval an an expiration date.

**Enforcement**

Violations may result in disciplinary action, and upto termination.

**Review**

This policy is reviewed at least annually and after any significant AI incident.
